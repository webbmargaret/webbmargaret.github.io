In this chapter, the authors propose a redefinition of ethical engagement within community-engaged scholarship, teaching, and learning. By leveraging their pedagogical experiences and extensive involvement in community-engaged initiatives both locally and globally, the authors contend that the dynamic landscape of global challenges necessitates a radical reevaluation of approaches to community engagement. They argue that such reevaluation is essential for scholars to effectively contribute to improving the communities in which they reside, work, and flourish. Furthermore, the authors prompt readers to reconsider the concept of reciprocity through an ethical lens, urging them to acknowledge the inherent imbalance in reciprocity that may arise from conflicting objectives between academic institutions and community stakeholders.
