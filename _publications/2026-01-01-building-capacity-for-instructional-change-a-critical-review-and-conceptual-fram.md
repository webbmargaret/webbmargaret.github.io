In this theory-focused full paper, we present a critical review connecting research on STEM postdoctoral associate professional development (PD) with scholarship on PD programs for building emerging Discipline-Based Education Research (DBER) scholars’ capacity for instructional change. Scholarship on STEM postdoc PD positions STEM postdocs as agents of change in STEM—an overlooked workforce expected to address national priorities and skill shortages—but we lack understanding of how postdoc agency, or capacity for change resulting from research, develops.  Based on our critical review, we present propositions for how DBER postdocs can develop capacity for instructional change resulting from DBER. This work offers an initial case for broader STEM postdoc PD building capacity for change resulting from research. We visualize our propositions into a conceptual model that can guide future development of postdoc-specific PD models and studies of how postdocs (and in turn, their fields) develop capacity for change.
