As Graduate Research Assistant for the VT College of Engineering Graduate and Professional Studies group (under Dr. Holly Matusovich), designed and implemented a professional development ecosystem for engineering graduate students. This work applied systems thinking to identify and address structural gaps in graduate student support, resulting in a suite of interconnected resources and workshop series reaching hundreds of students across the college.

Organized and hosted the Virginia Tech Dissertation Institute (VTDI), developed and managed the 2024–2025 workshop series for professional development (targeting graduate students and external fellowship holders), initiated a college-level Canvas site as a centralized repository for engineering graduate student resources, and developed a peer-mentoring program for external fellowship-holding students (GPS Student Ambassador Program).

## Workshop Series

**Poster Presentations for Research Conferences** *(April 2025, ~25 attendees)*
Best practices for designing and presenting research posters at academic and professional conferences.
[Slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1H5mHExAMMtINA1f75BtHK4v3N4z/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"}

**Getting the Most Out of Engineering Conferences** *(May 2025, ~25 attendees)*
Pre-conference preparation strategies, during-conference networking (including a "30-second research story" framework), and post-conference follow-up templates. Developed in collaboration with VT Center for Communicating Science.
[Slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1yPsntjBFgXrRqDkgP3rBKzqKgB4puApQ/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"}

**Planning to Apply for Engineering Graduate School Fellowships** *(September 2024, ~25 attendees)*
Strategies for researching and planning fellowship applications.
[Slides](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DqJMzOi967xslEz1GX-UIa_BhjVJgJw-/view?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"}

**Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion in Engineering and Natural Hazards Research** *(co-developed)*
Workshop using Mentimeter polls, breakout discussions, and structured reflection prompts to engage participants with concepts of bias, social vulnerability, and ethical decision-making in engineering research contexts.
[Lesson Plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K0gwI_fAVm9k_xXo96kHssifs-Yvyk5O/edit?usp=sharing){:target="_blank"}

## Dissertation Timeline Tool and Canvas Site

Developed a dissertation timeline planning tool incorporating "Absurdly Small Units" (ASUs) — a strategy for breaking dissertation writing into 5–10 minute tasks to address perfectionism and momentum challenges common among doctoral students. The tool helps students develop metacognitive awareness of their writing processes and create concrete, achievable goals.
