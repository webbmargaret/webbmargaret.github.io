Undergraduate learning assistant supporting a Solids Mechanics course in the Mechanical Engineering Department. Responsible for facilitating recitation sessions and collaborating with faculty instructors on iterative improvements to lab materials to better support student understanding.

This early teaching role grounded my belief that **assessment and curriculum materials should be continuously improved** based on how students are actually engaging with content — a principle that has remained central to my teaching philosophy across contexts.
