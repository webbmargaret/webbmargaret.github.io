Series of invited guest lectures for the Virginia Tech Rising Sophomore Abroad Program, designed to prepare rising sophomore engineering students for a summer trip exploring engineering workplaces and education systems internationally. Developed and delivered sessions on engineering work contexts, cross-cultural professional communication, and organizational culture frameworks. Reached approximately 40 students per session.

## Course Sessions

**"Engineering Work Abroad"** — Students analyzed authentic international engineering scenarios (e.g., designing retail spaces in Milan, managing rail projects in New Zealand) to identify stakeholders and consider local community contexts and cultural protocols. Students first examined their own cultural assumptions before analyzing other national contexts, helping them interrogate taken-for-granted beliefs prior to engaging with difference.

**"Culture in Engineering"** — Framed through [Hofstede's cultural dimensions](https://www.hofstede-insights.com/models/national-culture/){:target="_blank"} and Schein's organizational culture framework, this session engaged students with how culture operates at multiple system scales — national, organizational, disciplinary, and generational — to shape engineering practice. Students moved beyond technical problem-solving to develop social skills essential for professional interactions.

Both sessions incorporated **Miro boards** for community-building, check-ins, and reflective writing at the start of class, creating space for co-construction and making visible the diverse perspectives students brought.

## Assessment Design

I also developed an assessment framework for RSAP at a time when no formal program-level assessment existed for these sessions, designing formative and summative instruments — including a Global Competency Scenarios assessment, Cultural Intelligence Survey, STEPS instrument, and peer evaluations — as interconnected elements tied to [Fink's Significant Learning Taxonomy](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Creating+Significant+Learning+Experiences%3A+An+Integrated+Approach+to+Designing+College+Courses%2C+Revised+and+Updated+Edition-p-9781118124253){:target="_blank"} to ensure alignment with specified learning outcomes.
