Designed a graduate-level independent study on Critical Displacement Ethics in Engineering Education. Developed a complete independent study syllabus and reading list, articulating clear learning objectives and aligning activities to authentic assessment through manuscript development and peer review.

The manuscript served triple-duty as the course's primary assessment vehicle: formative assessment via regular instructor feedback, an authentic reflective exercise enabling applied learning, and a means of peer assessment — a key element of academic professional practice. This integrated assessment design reflects my broader philosophy that assignments, courses, and assessments should function as interconnected elements of a purposeful system, not isolated evaluation events.
