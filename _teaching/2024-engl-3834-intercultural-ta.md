Graduate teaching assistant for an undergraduate technical communication course focused on intercultural issues in professional writing, supporting instruction in Spring 2024 and Fall 2025. Taught course modules on participatory action research and community-engaged technical communication, effective presentations for academic conferences, and graded weekly discussion posts and mid-semester projects.

## Teaching Modules

**Participatory Action and Community-Engaged Technical Communication** — Drawing on my multi-year participatory action research with refugee communities and hospital educators across Virginia, I taught students how engineering and technical solutions must consider diverse stakeholder perspectives — often beyond immediate circles — to design more accessible applications, websites, instruction manuals, and other resources. During this unit, students engaged in place-based research projects, working in groups to redesign accessible interfaces based on community feedback, with me serving as a consultant and judge.

**Conference Presentations for Technical Communicators** — Module on planning for academic conferences and designing effective research posters, helping students develop professional communication skills for STEM contexts. This included sessions on communicating technical ideas to diverse audiences prior to structured poster judging.

## Related Work
This teaching experience connects directly to my participatory action research on digital literacy and community technology design with newcomers and hospital educators in Virginia.
